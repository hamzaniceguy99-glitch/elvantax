The programs, in detail

Four programs of 4 to 10 weeks, worked on your own transactions and invoices.

Program 01 · Thirty minutes a week

A system that sticks

Most freelancers don't need complicated accounting software; they need a separate bank account, a sensible category list and a recurring half hour. We set that up on whatever tool fits — spreadsheet included — and get your last few months entered so you start from current.

  • 8 weeks
  • Freelancers
  • 30 min a week
  • Bank statements

Program 02 · Findable a year later

Receipts & records

We set up capture that happens at the moment of purchase, not in a panic later, plus logs for mileage and other recurring records, sensible file naming, backups, and how long to keep things. What is and isn't deductible is a question for your tax professional — we make sure the records exist so they can answer it.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 30 min a week
  • A phone camera

Program 03 · Cash, not just revenue

Invoicing & getting paid

Plenty of profitable freelancers run out of cash. We build clear invoices, deposits and payment terms, a chasing sequence that is polite and firm and automatic, and a simple cash view so you know what's actually coming in this month.

  • 6 weeks
  • Invoicing clients
  • 1 hour a week
  • Your invoice template

Weeks 1–2 · Reconciling

Program 04 · Make your accountant cheap

Quarterly & handoff

Clean books cost less to process and produce fewer surprises. We build a quarterly rhythm — reconcile, review, set money aside for obligations your professional tells you about — and a year-end package your accountant can open and use. Everything tax-specific goes to them; we prepare nothing and file nothing.

  • Books running
  • 2 hours a week
